## Our Properties

### Perfume Picks — Fragrance Discovery (iOS, live on the App Store)

A native iOS app for fragrance enthusiasts who research, collect, and buy perfumes and
colognes. Users build a personal scent wardrobe, train a taste profile through a swipe-based
preference engine, and decide which bottle to buy next.

- **100% fragrance-intent audience** — no off-topic dilution.
- **Buy links at peak intent** — surfaced only after a user has opened a specific
  fragrance and read its notes, accords, longevity, and projection.
- **Repeat buyers** — wardrobe tracking (own / want / tried) creates natural reorder,
  upgrade, decant, and gifting moments.
- **Catalog depth** — thousands of designer and niche fragrances, continuously updated
  from live retailer feeds, so deep links resolve to real product pages.

**Ideal advertiser partners:** designer and niche fragrance retailers with broad catalogs
and reliable product feeds.

### ITIN Credit Score · ITIN Lending · ITIN Credit Card — Financial Access Suite

A suite of properties serving ITIN holders — individuals who file US taxes with an
Individual Taxpayer Identification Number rather than an SSN. This is a large, underserved,
and historically hard-to-reach audience that is actively seeking mainstream financial
products and is frequently turned away by tools that assume an SSN.

- **ITIN Credit Score** — helps ITIN holders understand, monitor, and build credit.
- **ITIN Lending** — connects ITIN holders with lenders and loan products that accept an ITIN.
- **ITIN Credit Card** — surfaces credit cards and approval paths available to ITIN applicants.

This audience converts because intent is explicit: a user arrives specifically looking for
a credit card, a loan, or a way to build credit they can actually qualify for. We route
that intent to advertisers who serve ITIN and thin-file applicants.

**Ideal advertiser partners:** banks, card issuers, lenders, credit-monitoring and
credit-repair services — especially those that approve ITIN, immigrant, or thin-file applicants.
