About Perfume Picks

An iPhone app for a fragrance wardrobe, built and run by one person.

Quick Answer

Perfume Picks is an iPhone app for keeping track of a fragrance collection, and I build and run it on my own. It files the bottles you own, logs every wear and picks a scent each morning from your own wardrobe. It doesn't sell fragrance. It makes money from an optional Pro subscription and retailer affiliate links.

Who runs Perfume Picks?

I'm Bob Guillow. I build and run Perfume Picks on my own, and it's published under Timberline Ventures LLC, my own company. There's no outside investor, ad network or parent company, so I make the product and privacy decisions myself, and I'm the one who answers support@perfumepicks.app.

What it is

Perfume Picks is an iPhone app for fragrance collectors. It files your wardrobe with a remaining-mL meter and what you paid for each bottle, logs every wear with the occasion, the weather and a rating, builds a Fragrance DNA profile from the bottles you love and the swipes you make, and picks a Scent of the Day from your own wardrobe. A dupe finder shows cheaper bottles that smell close, and Perfume Wrapped recaps your year.

What it is not

  • It doesn't sell fragrance. Fragrance pages link to retailers, and I may earn a small commission if you buy through one.
  • It isn't a scraper of other fragrance sites. The catalog comes from commercial fragrance-data providers and manual curation.
  • It isn't a marketplace, so there are no listings, auctions or decant swaps.
  • There are no ads in the app or on this site.
  • I don't sell or share your data for advertising. The privacy policy has the details.

How it makes money

Perfume Picks is free to download. Pro is $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial. It removes the free limits on the wardrobe, wear log, scans and swipes, unlocks the full dupes list and adds Perfume Wrapped. Apple handles billing, so payment details never reach my servers.

Fragrance pages also have buy links to retailers. If you buy through one, I may earn a small commission. It's disclosed on the page and in the Terms of Service, and the price you pay is the same.

Why I built it

The big fragrance reference sites are built for looking a bottle up, and most newer apps are built for shopping. Perfume Picks is for the bottles already on the shelf: what you own, what you've worn, and what to wear today.

Editorial standards

Articles are written from collecting experience and primary sources like manufacturer notes, perfumer interviews and IFRA documentation. Facts link to their sources and opinions are labeled. The updated date only changes when an article does.

Contact

Press, partnerships and feedback all go to support@perfumepicks.app. I usually reply within a day.
