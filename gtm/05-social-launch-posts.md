## REPLY / ENGAGEMENT GUARDRAILS

- Reply like a person who knows fragrance, not a support bot. Recommend honestly, even a competitor scent.
- Never argue pricing. "Fair — the DNA, wardrobe, and daily picks are free for good."
- When someone names a bottle we're missing: "Adding it — the catalog grows weekly. Thanks for the flag."
- Never fake numbers or social proof. No "join thousands."
- One emoji max. No hashtag walls except where the platform rewards them (TikTok/IG, 3–5 relevant tags).
