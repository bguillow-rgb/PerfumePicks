Features

What the app does, screen by screen.

Scent of the Day

What should I wear today?

Every morning the app picks a bottle from your own wardrobe. It goes on your Fragrance DNA, the local weather, the occasion and what you haven’t worn in a while, and it gives you one line on why.

One tap logs the wear. You can turn on a daily reminder if you want one.

Your wardrobe

Every bottle by house and family, marked Have, Want or Tested. A remaining-mL meter on each bottle drops as you log wears, and you can record what you paid.

The free version holds 5 bottles. Pro has no limit.

Fragrance DNA

Train My Nose

Tap up to five bottles you love and the app builds your Fragrance DNA from their accords and names your archetype. Train My Nose then shows you fragrances to swipe on, three a day on the free version, and the profile sharpens with every swipe and every wear you log.

A taste profile screen shows the signals behind it.

Find your next bottle

Discover has curated edits by mood, Celebrity Picks, new arrivals and a browse by house, and you can compare two fragrances side by side.

Every fragrance page has buy links to retailers. I may earn a small commission if you use one, and the price is the same.

Fragrance Detail

Every note, every wear

Tap any fragrance to see its notes and accords, your own wear history and a private My Notes section. You can rate and review it, and read other people’s reviews.

Open the dupes on any fragrance to see cheaper bottles ranked by how close they smell, with what you’d save. Free shows the closest one and Pro shows the rest.

The wear log and Perfume Wrapped

Each wear records the date, the occasion, the weather, a rating and a note, and you can log a layered combination of two bottles. The free version keeps 10 wear logs.

At the end of the year, Perfume Wrapped recaps your own wear log. It’s a Pro feature.
