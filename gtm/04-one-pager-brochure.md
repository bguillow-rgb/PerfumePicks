### [HERO STATEMENT]

# Wear what you love.

Most fragrance apps want to sell you the next bottle. Perfume Picks works from the ones already on your shelf — a wardrobe, a wear log, and a taste engine for people who collect.

---

### [THE PROBLEM] — *Sound familiar?*

You own dozens of bottles and wear the same three. You forget what's on the back of the shelf. You can't remember which scent got the compliments, or whether the expensive one is actually expensive once you count the wears. And every app you try just wants to sell you more.

---

### [WHAT IT DOES] — four pillars

**Your wardrobe, organized.**
Every bottle in one place — have, want, worn, tested, sold — with remaining mL, price, and last-worn date.

**A wear log that remembers.**
Tap to log a wear. It captures the weather, asks the occasion, and builds your history, your compliments, your cost-per-wear.

**A pick for tonight, not just shopping.**
A daily Scent of the Day from your own bottles — weather-aware, occasion-aware, nudging the neglected ones.

**A taste profile that's truly yours.**
Your Fragrance DNA — one of 11 archetypes — built from what you pick, swipe, and wear. And it keeps sharpening.

---

### [THE SIGNATURE FEATURE]

## Discover your Fragrance DNA

Tap the scents you love and Perfume Picks reads your palate into one of **11 archetypes** — The Connoisseur, The Seducer, The Purist, The Showstopper, The Smart Shopper, The Rebel, and more. It's your taste, named — and it's **Living**, sharpening every time you use the app.

---

### [THE VALUE FEATURE]

## Cheaper bottles that smell the same

Love a $300 fragrance? Perfume Picks surfaces dupes that smell nearly identical, ranked by match and showing the savings — **often 30–70% less**. One good find pays for a year of Pro.

---

### [MORE]

Scan a bottle to add it · Cost-per-wear analytics · Layering suggestions · Compliments log · "Who Wears This" verified celebrity associations · Perfume Wrapped (your year in scent)

### [THE PROMISE]

**No ads. We don't sell or share your data. Your wardrobe stays yours.**
Perfume Picks is not a retailer — we never sell fragrance. When you choose to buy, you buy from real retailers, and we may earn a commission.
