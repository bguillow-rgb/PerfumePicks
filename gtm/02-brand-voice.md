# Brand Voice

The guardrails. If a piece of copy doesn't sound like this, rewrite it.

## Who we sound like

A well-dressed friend who happens to know fragrance cold — confident, warm, a little dry. Never a salesperson, never a hype account, never an AI assistant. The voice of someone who owns a real collection and respects that you do too.

## Three voice traits

1. **Confident, not loud.** We don't shout. We state. "Wear what you love." Short declaratives over exclamation points. The category screams discounts; we whisper taste.
2. **Editorial, not technical.** We write like a good fragrance column, not a spec sheet. "Skin-close scents that whisper instead of shout" — not "low-sillage formulations."
3. **Honest, not salesy.** We name the limits ("often 30–70% less," not "always"). We say plainly that we're not a store. Trust is the brand.

## Tone by surface

- **App Store:** clear and benefit-first. Lead with what it does for *you*.
- **Social:** human and specific. Sound like a collector texting a thought, not a brand posting a thing.
- **Ads:** one idea, one image, one line. No feature lists.
- **Press:** factual, founder-led, anti-hype. Let the inversion (own vs. buy) carry it.
- **Email:** like a note from Bob, not a newsletter blast.

## Vocabulary

**Use:** wardrobe, collection, bottles, the shelf, wear (verb + noun), Fragrance DNA, archetype, pick, scent of the day, accords, notes, house, layering, compliments, cost-per-wear, dupe.

**Avoid:** "users," "leverage," "unlock your potential," "game-changer," "revolutionary," "AI-powered" (we use AI; we don't brag about it), "shop now," "limited time," excessive emoji, hashtag stacks.

## The lines we own (use verbatim)

- The Fragrance Collector's Wardrobe.
- Wear what you love.
- Picks for tonight, not just shopping.
- A taste profile from your collection.
- The back of the shelf gets its turn.
- No ads. Your wardrobe stays yours.
- We're not a store.

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Get a morning pick from bottles you already own." | "Discover amazing new fragrances to buy!" |
| "Your taste, named: one of 11 archetypes." | "Take our fun quiz!!! 🎉" |
| "Dupes that smell nearly identical — often 30–70% less." | "Save BIG on perfume dupes!!!" |
| "No ads. We don't sell your data." | "We value your privacy." (limp) |
| "Built by a collector." | "Trusted by thousands." (untrue pre-launch) |

## Punctuation & formatting

- Em dashes for the editorial aside. Periods over exclamation points.
- Sentence case in UI and most headlines. Title Case only for the tagline and archetype names.
- One emoji max, and only when it earns its place. Usually zero.
- Never invent numbers. No stats we can't stand behind.

## The voice test

Before publishing, ask: *Would a serious collector screenshot this and send it to a friend, or roll their eyes?* If it reads like an engagement bot or a discount banner, it fails.
