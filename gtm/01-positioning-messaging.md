# Positioning & Messaging

The strategic foundation. Every brochure, post, ad, and email derives from this document.

## The market problem

The fragrance-app market is split in two, and neither half serves the collector:

- **Retailers** (Scentbird, FragranceX, Sephora) exist to sell you the *next* bottle. They have no idea what's already on your shelf.
- **Reference sites** (Fragrantica) are encyclopedias — dated, cluttered, built for lookups, not for living with a collection.

So the person who already owns 15, 40, 80 bottles is stuck. They forget what they have. The same three scents get worn while the back of the shelf goes untouched. They can't remember which one got compliments, what they wore to that wedding last June, or whether the $300 bottle is actually expensive once you count the wears. And every "fragrance app" just wants to sell them more.

## The wedge

**Perfume Picks is the first app built for the bottles you already own.** A wardrobe, a wear log, and a taste engine — not a store. That single inversion ("wear what you love" vs. "buy what's next") is the whole brand.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** The established collector. Dozens of bottles. Knows their accords from their notes. Follows fragrance YouTube/Reddit. Has opinions about batch codes. Wants to *use* the collection they've built, and quietly enjoys being understood by something that isn't trying to upsell them.

**Secondary:** The growing enthusiast (5–15 bottles) leveling up from "I like this one" to a real wardrobe and a point of view.

**Not the target (but welcome):** The total beginner. There's an on-ramp, but the marketing speaks to people who already have a shelf.

## Positioning statement

> For fragrance collectors who own more than they wear, **Perfume Picks** is the iOS wardrobe app that turns your collection into daily picks, a wear history, and a personal Fragrance DNA — because unlike every other fragrance app, it works from the bottles you already own, not the ones a store wants to sell you.

## The four value pillars

1. **Your wardrobe, finally organized.** Every bottle in one place — have, want, worn, tested, sold — with remaining mL, price, and last-worn date. The back of the shelf gets its turn.
2. **A wear log that remembers.** Tap to log a wear; it captures the weather and asks the occasion. Over time it knows your compliments, your cost-per-wear, your patterns.
3. **A pick for tonight, not just shopping.** A morning Scent of the Day drawn from *your* bottles — weather-aware, occasion-aware, nudging the ones you've neglected.
4. **A taste profile that's truly yours.** Your Fragrance DNA — one of 11 archetypes — built from what you pick, swipe, and wear, and it keeps sharpening every time you open the app.

## The two headline features (lead with these)

### Fragrance DNA + archetypes (the identity hook)
Tap the bottles you love, and the app reads your palate into one of **11 archetypes** — The Executive, The Seducer, The Connoisseur, The Purist, The Showstopper, The Smart Shopper, The Romantic, The Explorer, The Classicist, The Rebel, The Signature Wearer. It's specific, it's flattering in an honest way, and it's *shareable* — the kind of result people screenshot. And it's **Living**: it re-ranks as you use the app, so your DNA in month three is sharper than day one.

> Marketing note: lead with Fragrance DNA / archetypes as the centerpiece. "Train My Nose" (the swipe interface) is the *mechanism that feeds it*, not the headline.

### Budget Dupes (the value hook)
Found a $300 bottle you love? The app surfaces cheaper alternatives that smell nearly identical, ranked by match % and showing the dollars saved — **often 30–70% less.** One good find pays for a year of Pro. This is the easiest paid conversion argument we have.

## The full feature set (for body copy and feature lists)

- **Fragrance DNA & 11 archetypes** — your taste, named.
- **Living DNA** — the profile keeps sharpening with every wear and swipe.
- **Wardrobe** — have / want / worn / tested / sold, with remaining mL and price.
- **Wear log** — auto weather + date, occasion, spray count; backdate up to 2 years; log layering combos.
- **Scent of the Day** — a morning pick from your own bottles, weather- and occasion-aware.
- **Bottle Scan** — photograph a bottle, AI identifies it and adds it to your wardrobe.
- **Budget Dupes** — cheaper near-identical alternatives, ranked, with savings.
- **Cost-per-wear** — the "expensive" daily driver turns out to be the cheap one.
- **Train My Nose** — swipe to teach the app your taste.
- **Who Wears This** — verified celebrity associations on each fragrance.
- **Perfume Wrapped** — your year in scent, as a shareable card.
- **Layering suggestions & compliments log** — what to combine, what works.

## Free vs. Pro (the conversion story)

**Free** is genuinely useful — Fragrance DNA reveal, wardrobe (up to 20 bottles), wear log, daily pick, a few dupes, ~10 swipes/day, daily scans. The DNA reveal is never paywalled; it's the activation hook.

**Pro ($2.99/mo or $24.99/yr, 7-day trial on annual)** unlocks: Living DNA + full profile, unlimited wardrobe, all dupes, unlimited swipes, layering suggestions, Perfume Wrapped, advanced analytics & cost-per-wear.

**The pitch:** *"The only app that learns your taste into a Fragrance DNA — then finds you cheaper bottles that smell just as good."*

## Proof points & trust

- **Independent & private.** No ads. We don't sell or share your data. Your wardrobe stays yours.
- **Not a retailer.** We never sell fragrance. When you buy, you buy from real retailers (FragranceX, Sephora, Amazon Luxury Beauty); we may earn a commission, disclosed on the page.
- **Built by a collector,** Bob Guillow — solo, independent, for people like him.
- **iOS-first,** designed to match the luxury fragrance category: clean, warm, boutique.

## Competitive one-liners (for sales/press)

- vs. **Scentbird/FragranceX:** "They sell you the next bottle. We help you wear the ones you have."
- vs. **Fragrantica:** "An encyclopedia you look things up in vs. a wardrobe you actually live in."
- vs. **a notes app / spreadsheet:** "A spreadsheet can't pick tonight's scent for the weather, or find you a dupe."

## Message hierarchy (what to say, in order)

1. **Hook:** The collector's wardrobe — wear what you love.
2. **Identity:** Discover your Fragrance DNA (one of 11 archetypes).
3. **Value:** Find dupes that smell the same for 30–70% less.
4. **Utility:** A daily pick, a wear log, your whole collection organized.
5. **Trust:** No ads. Not a store. Your data stays yours.
