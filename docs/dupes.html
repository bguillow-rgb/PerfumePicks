Fragrance dupes

Perfume Picks tracks 74 documented alternatives across 60 fragrances. Every pairing carries a match percentage and a written rationale, so you can see why two scents are considered close rather than taking a list's word for it.

A dupe matches a fragrance's overall accord direction, not its exact formula. Projection, longevity and the dry-down usually differ, which is why each guide below compares the full note pyramid and community wear scores side by side instead of just naming a cheaper bottle.
