# Perfume Picks — Go-To-Market Kit

The launch playbook and ready-to-use marketing collateral for **Perfume Picks** — *The Fragrance Collector's Wardrobe.*

Everything here is grounded in the shipping product (see `docs/APP-CURRENT-STATE.md` and `web/src/consts.ts`), not invented. Copy is written to be pasted into App Store Connect, social schedulers, ad managers, and press outreach with minimal edits.

## Product in one line

An iOS app that turns a fragrance collection into a smart wardrobe: catalog every bottle, log each wear with weather and occasion, get a morning pick from scents **you already own**, build a personal **Fragrance DNA**, and find cheaper bottles that smell nearly identical.

## The single source of truth (do not contradict)

- **Publisher:** Timberline Ventures LLC · Founder: Bob Guillow

- **Palette:** warm ivory `#FAF6F0`, champagne gold `#B8924B`, ink brown `#2A1F18`.

## The non-negotiables (legal + brand)

1. **We are not a retailer.** We never sell fragrance. We link out to retailers and may earn an affiliate commission. Say this plainly wherever shopping is mentioned.
2. **No ads. We don't sell or share your data.** This is a core promise — repeat it, don't dilute it.
3. **No invented metrics.** Until the app is live there are no install/rating numbers. Don't fabricate "10,000 collectors love it." Use product truth instead.
4. **Dupe savings are "often 30–70% less," not guarantees.** Keep the hedge.
