Features

What it does

Perfume Picks is an iPhone app I built for keeping track of a fragrance collection. It files the bottles you own, logs each wear with the weather and the occasion, and picks something to wear each morning from your own wardrobe. A short taste picker builds your Fragrance DNA, and a dupe finder shows cheaper bottles that smell close. It doesn't sell fragrance.

Your wardrobe, filed. Every bottle by house and family, marked Have, Want or Tested, with how much is left in it and what you paid.

A wear log. Tap to log a wear with the date, the occasion, the weather and a rating. The bottle’s remaining-mL meter drops as you go.

Scent of the Day. A pick from your own wardrobe each morning, with a line on why it fits today.

Fragrance DNA. Tap the bottles you love and swipe through Train My Nose. The profile sharpens with every wear you log.

Fragrance DNA

Your taste, named.

Tap the scents you love and Perfume Picks reads them into a Fragrance DNA archetype. It sharpens every time you log a wear or swipe in Train My Nose.

The Signature Wearer, The Smart Shopper and The Connoisseur are three of them.

Budget dupes

Cheaper bottles that smell close

Open any fragrance and Perfume Picks lists its dupes, ranked by how close they smell, with what you’d save, usually 30 to 70%.

The free version shows the closest dupe. Pro shows the rest of the list.

Pro

How does Perfume Picks make money?

Perfume Picks is free to download. Pro is $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial. It removes the free limits, unlocks the full dupes list and adds Perfume Wrapped, a yearly recap of your own wears. Fragrance pages also have buy links to retailers, and I may earn a small commission if you use one. There are no ads, and I don’t sell your data.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Perfume Picks?

It's an iPhone app I built to keep track of a fragrance wardrobe. You file the bottles you own, log each wear, and every morning the app picks a Scent of the Day from your own wardrobe. A short taste picker learns what you like.

Is Perfume Picks a retailer?

No. Perfume Picks doesn't sell fragrance. Fragrance pages have buy links to retailers, and I may earn a small commission if you use one. The price you pay is the same, and the Terms spell this out.

Is Perfume Picks free?

Yes. The free version holds 5 bottles, 5 label scans, 10 wear logs and 3 Train My Nose swipes a day, and it shows the closest dupe for any fragrance. Pro is $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial. It removes those limits, unlocks the full dupes list and adds Perfume Wrapped. Apple handles billing, and you can cancel any time in iOS Settings.

How does the wear log work?

Open a fragrance and tap Log a Wear. You set the date, the occasion, the weather and a rating, and you can add a note. Each wear drops the bottle's remaining-mL meter. You can also log a layered combination of two bottles.

How does Scent of the Day work?

Each morning the app picks a bottle from your wardrobe. It goes on your Fragrance DNA, the local weather, the occasion and what you haven't worn lately, and it gives you one line on why. One tap logs the wear.

Is my wardrobe data private?

Yes. Your wardrobe, wear log, ratings and notes are tied to your account. I don't sell them or use them for advertising. The Privacy Policy covers what the community Scent of the Day feed shows.

Will there be an Android version?

Not yet. Perfume Picks is iPhone only for now.
