## Press release

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

### Perfume Picks launches the first iOS app built for the bottles you already own

*A fragrance "wardrobe" that logs your wears, picks tonight's scent, and learns your taste into a Fragrance DNA — without trying to sell you anything*

**[CITY, DATE]** — Perfume Picks, an independent iOS app for fragrance collectors, is now available on the App Store. Unlike the fragrance apps collectors have today — which are either retailers built to sell the next bottle or aging reference databases — Perfume Picks works entirely from the bottles a person already owns.

The app turns a fragrance collection into a usable wardrobe. Collectors catalog every bottle (have, want, worn, tested, sold), log each wear with the weather and occasion automatically captured, and open the app each day to a "Scent of the Day" drawn from their own shelf — weather- and occasion-aware, and biased toward the bottles they've been neglecting.

Its signature feature is **Fragrance DNA**: by tapping the scents they love, users get their taste read into one of 11 archetypes — The Connoisseur, The Seducer, The Purist, The Showstopper, and others. The profile is "Living," sharpening continuously as the user wears, swipes, and edits their collection.

For collectors mindful of cost, Perfume Picks surfaces **dupes** — cheaper fragrances that smell nearly identical — ranked by match and showing the savings, often 30–70% less than the original.

"Every fragrance app I tried just wanted to sell me the next bottle," said Bob Guillow, founder of Perfume Picks. "I had a shelf full of fragrances I loved and kept forgetting about. I wanted something that helped me wear what I already own — so I built it."

Perfume Picks is free to start, including the Fragrance DNA reveal, the wardrobe, the wear log, and daily picks. Perfume Picks Pro ($2.99/month or $24.99/year with a 7-day free trial) unlocks the Living Fragrance DNA, an unlimited wardrobe, all dupes, layering suggestions, Perfume Wrapped, and advanced cost-per-wear analytics.

The app carries no advertising and does not sell or share user data. It is not a retailer and never sells fragrance; when a user chooses to buy, they purchase from established retailers, and Perfume Picks may earn an affiliate commission, disclosed on the page.

Perfume Picks is available now on the App Store for iPhone. Learn more at perfumepicks.app.

**Media contact:** Bob Guillow · support@perfumepicks.app · perfumepicks.app

## Key features (for the "Features" sidebar)

- Fragrance DNA — taste read into one of 11 archetypes; Living, keeps sharpening
- Wardrobe — have / want / worn / tested / sold, with mL + price
- Wear log — auto weather + date, occasion, backdating, layering
- Scent of the Day — a daily pick from your own bottles

- Bottle Scan — photograph a bottle to add it

## Approved talking points / angles for journalists

1. **The inversion:** the first fragrance app for the bottles you *own*, not the ones a store wants to sell.
