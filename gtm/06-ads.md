# Advertising

Apple Search Ads keyword plan and paid social copy. Per the ASO playbook: Search Ads is both the primary paid surface *and* the keyword-research engine — bid broad to discover, move winners into the keyword field, scale on exact.

---

## Apple Search Ads

### Account structure

- **Campaign 1 — Brand (defensive):** bid on `perfume picks` and close variants. Cheap, protects branded search.
- **Campaign 2 — Category Discovery (Broad):** broad match to mine real search terms. Harvest converters weekly into exact + the keyword field.
- **Campaign 3 — Category Scale (Exact):** the proven converters from Discovery, bid up.
- **Campaign 4 — Competitor (where Apple allows):** `scentbird`, `fragrantica`, `fragrancex` — expect higher CPA; run small, judge on CVR.

### Seed keyword list (Discovery → graduate winners to Exact)

**High intent / wardrobe-collection:**
fragrance collection, perfume collection, cologne collection, fragrance tracker, perfume tracker, cologne tracker, fragrance inventory, perfume organizer, fragrance wardrobe, fragrance journal, perfume diary, fragrance log, scent diary

**Dupe / value:**
perfume dupes, fragrance dupes, cologne dupes, perfume clones, fragrance clones, cheap perfume, perfume alternatives

**Discovery / taste:**
fragrance finder, perfume quiz, find my scent, fragrance recommendations, scent matcher, fragrance test

**Catalog / reference:**
fragrance notes, perfume notes, fragrance database, cologne app, perfume app, niche fragrance

### Negative keywords (add immediately)

free perfume, perfume samples free, perfume coupon, fake perfume, perfume jobs, perfume wholesale, sephora, ulta, scentbird login, [any pure-retail purchase intent that won't convert to an app install]

> Note: keep `scentbird`/`fragrancex` ONLY in the dedicated Competitor campaign; negative them everywhere else.

### Custom Product Pages (CPPs) to pair with ad groups

- **CPP-DNA** (hero: archetype reveal) → for `quiz / finder / recommendations` ad groups.
- **CPP-Dupes** (hero: dupe list w/ savings) → for `dupes / clones / cheap` ad groups.
- **CPP-Wardrobe** (hero: collection grid) → for `collection / tracker / organizer` ad groups.

Match the ad's promise to the page's first screenshot — this is the single biggest CVR lever.

## Paid Social — Meta (Instagram / Facebook)

One idea per ad. Static = real UI screenshot + one champagne-gold line on ivory. Video = the preview-video beat sheet.

**Ad 1 — Identity (DNA) [cold, interest: fragrance/perfume]**
- Primary text: *Tap the bottles you love. Perfume Picks names your taste — one of 11 fragrance archetypes. Your taste, finally named.*
- Headline: **What's your Fragrance DNA?**
- CTA: Download
- Creative: archetype reveal card

**Ad 2 — Value (Dupes) [cold/retarget, broad + value-shoppers]**
- Primary text: *Love a $300 bottle? There's probably a dupe that smells nearly identical for 30–70% less. Perfume Picks ranks them by match and shows the savings.*
- Headline: **Same scent. A fraction of the price.**
- CTA: Download
- Creative: dupe list with match % + savings

**Ad 3 — Utility (Wardrobe / SOTD) [cold, collectors]**
- Primary text: *You own dozens of bottles and wear the same three. Perfume Picks organizes your whole collection and picks tonight's scent for the weather — from what you already own.*
- Headline: **Wear what you love.**
- CTA: Download
- Creative: Today / Scent of the Day screen

**Ad 4 — Trust / founder [warm, retarget]**
- Primary text: *No ads. We don't sell your data. Not a store. Just a wardrobe for the bottles you already own — built by a collector.*
- Headline: **The fragrance app that isn't a store.**
- CTA: Download

## Paid Social — TikTok (Spark Ads / in-feed)

Use the organic TikTok script from `05-social-launch-posts.md` as the base — UGC-style outperforms polished. Three variants to test:
1. **DNA reveal hook** — "I let an app read my fragrance taste."
2. **Dupe hook** — "It found a dupe of my $300 bottle."
3. **Collection-shame hook** — "How many of your bottles have you not touched in 6 months?"

Keep hooks in the first 2 seconds. Caption ends on "Perfume Picks. Free on iOS."

## Creative briefs (for a designer)

- **Format:** real in-app screenshots, never stock. Hybrid (UI + one bold caption).
- **Palette:** ivory `#FAF6F0` background, champagne gold `#B8924B` headline, ink brown `#2A1F18` body. Cormorant Garamond for the headline line.
- **One message per asset.** No feature lists in ads.
- **Sizes:** 1080×1080 (feed), 1080×1920 (Stories/Reels/TikTok), App Store 6.7" + 6.5" screenshot sets.

## Measurement

- North-star paid metric: **cost per install → cost per Pro trial start.** A dupe-led ad can justify a higher CPI because the dupe value prop converts to Pro fastest.
- Run a 2-week test before judging any creative (per ASO playbook).
- Weekly: pull Search Ads search-term report → graduate winners to Exact + keyword field, add negatives. Offer to schedule this loop once spend is live.
- **Do not** fabricate ROAS in reporting; report real install/trial numbers only.
