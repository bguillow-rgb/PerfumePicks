Common Questions

How do I add a fragrance to my wardrobe?

Tap the plus button on the Wardrobe tab and search by fragrance or house, or point the camera at the bottle's label. Pick the right entry, mark it Have, Want or Tested, and add the bottle size, how much is left and what you paid if you want to. The free version holds 5 bottles and 5 label scans.

Why doesn’t my fragrance appear in search?

The catalog has more than 13,000 fragrances, but it isn't complete. If one is missing, use the Suggest a Fragrance link at the bottom of the search results and include the house, the name, the year if you know it and a source link. I add suggestions in the next catalog update.

How do I cancel my Perfume Picks Pro subscription?

Apple manages the subscription. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, then tap Perfume Picks Pro and Cancel Subscription. It stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting your account doesn't cancel the subscription.

What does Pro add?

The free version holds 5 bottles, 5 label scans, 10 wear logs and 3 Train My Nose swipes a day, and shows the closest dupe for any fragrance. Pro is $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year with a 7-day free trial. It removes those limits, unlocks the full dupes list and adds Perfume Wrapped.

What data does Perfume Picks collect?

Your sign-in identity from Apple or Google, the wardrobe and wear-log entries you create, basic device diagnostics and anonymous usage analytics. Scent of the Day looks up the local weather from your IP address. I don't collect health, contacts or financial data, and Apple handles payments. The Privacy Policy has the details.

How do I delete my Perfume Picks account?

Open the app, go to the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete Account and confirm. That permanently deletes your wardrobe, wear log, swipe history, taste profile, ratings and reviews, and it can't be undone. If you can't get into the app, the Delete Account page on this site explains how to request it by email.

How do I contact support?

Email support@perfumepicks.app with any question, bug or feature request. Include your account email, your iOS version and your app version (it's at the bottom of the Profile tab) and I can track down the problem faster. I answer every email myself, usually within a day.
